Traits

Draconic Essence. Chosen fresh each time you summon the drake: acid, cold, fire, lightning, or poison. That choice sets the drake's damage immunity and the damage type of its Infused Strikes, Bite, and Magic/Empowered Fang dice. The drake's fire-forged gear below assumes Fire is the essence most often chosen for this bond — but by the rules, it can be any of the five each time it's called.

Wings of the Bond. While summoned, the drake has a flying speed equal to its walking speed.

Drake Mount. The drake is Large-sized. You may ride it as a mount if you are Medium or smaller.

Magic Fang. The drake's Bite deals an extra 2d6 damage of its Draconic Essence type.

Empathic Bond. You and the drake are always empathically linked, on any distance apart as long as you share a plane. Nothing can sever it while you are on the same plane. Each of you can sense the other's basic emotions and general state: fear, calm, anger, pain, and the like.

Actions

Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit (+9 base, +3 Wraps of Unarmed Power), reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d6 + 9 piercing or force damage, your choice (base 1d6 + 6, +3 from the Wraps of Unarmed Power folded into the bite), plus 2d6 fire damage (Magic Fang / Empowered Bite), plus 1d6 fire damage that ignores resistance (Flame-Bound Strike, Draconic Rebirth).

Summon the Drake 1/Long Rest, or a Spell Slot Lyrielle action
As an action, summon the drake into an unoccupied space within 30 feet, choosing its Draconic Essence fresh. With Draconic Rebirth equipped, it takes its turn immediately after yours, no command needed. It remains until dropped to 0 HP, resummoned, or you die.

Drake's Breath 1/Long Rest, or 3rd-lvl+ Slot Lyrielle action
As an action, Lyrielle or her Drake exhale a 30 foot cone of elements. Choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison damage - the choice doesn't need to match your drake's Draconic Essence.
Dexterity Saving Throw: DC17, each creature in the Cone.
Failure: 10d6 damage of the chosen type.
Success: Half as much damage.

Recharge Recharge 6 Lyrielle feature
Once you've used Drake's Breath, roll a d6 at the start of each of your turns while in combat; on a 6, you regain the use of Drake's Breath immediately, without a rest or a spell slot. Becomes a 5–6 recharge during Inferno Ascendant. This only triggers during initiatve.

Shared Senses Lyrielle bonus action
See or hear through the drake's senses until the start of your next turn. While you do, you're blinded and/or deafened to your own surroundings — whichever of the drake's senses you're tapping into — and you lose your own sense of taste for the duration.

Reactions

Infused Strikes. When another creature within 30 feet that the drake can see hits a target with a weapon attack, the drake infuses the strike, adding 1d6 damage of its Draconic Essence type.

Reflexive Resistance 6/Long Rest Lyrielle reaction
When either you or the drake takes damage while you're within 30 feet of each other, you can use your reaction to give yourself or the drake resistance to that instance of damage. You can use this reaction a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

Carried Equipment

Green's Blessing Glory Scales equipped, attuned by Lyrielle

Wondrous item · requires attunement to a dragon

Glory Scales are a draconic fashion term for artificial scales added to a dragon to enhance their natural beauty and awe-inspiring majesty.

These Glory Scales are set with a pale green gem in a silver setting, however when attuned to, the setting changes color to match the dragon they are attuned to. While attuned to this item the dragon gains the following effects:

Mending Breath 1/Long Rest Lyrielle action
Once per day the dragon may replace their breath weapon with a 30 foot cone that heals for 8d8 + Wis Mod.

Innate Spellcasting. The Dragon gains an innate spellcasting ability if they do not have one already. The Dragon's innate spellcasting ability is Wisdom. They may cast the following spells requiring no material components:

  • At will: Guidance, Elementalism
  • 1/day each: Speak with Plants, Speak with Animals

Bonded Souls 1/Day magic action New reaction for both bonded creatures
Once per day, as a magic action, the Dragon may bond with another willing creature by touching it. While bonded with another creature, the dragon and that creature are resistant to all damage, but any damage done to one of them is also dealt to the other creature.

In addition when making a saving throw they may each choose to use the appropriate saving throw from the other creature instead. If one of the creatures is immune to a type of damage or condition both of them are. For the purposes of spells and other magical effects the pair of creatures count as one creature (healing one heals both, Hunter's Mark effects attack from both of them, and can also target both of them at the same time).

While bonded like this, either creature may, as a reaction, choose to take all the damage from an incoming damage source. This bond lasts 1 hour.

Draconic Rebirth equipped, attuned by Lyrielle

Requires attunement · worn by the drake

A tempered fragment of Varmyrr's strength, bound into the drake's hide. While worn:

  • +1 bonus to AC.
  • The drake takes its turn immediately after yours with no action required to command it. It is no longer limited to the Dodge action as default.
  • The drake gains bonus hit points equal to half your ranger level (+8 at 16th level).

Flame-Bound Strike. The drake's attacks carry controlled fire: its Bite deals an extra 1d6 fire damage that ignores resistance; its other natural attacks deal an extra 1d4 fire damage. On a critical hit, creatures the drake chooses within 5 feet make a DC 15 Dexterity save or take 1d6 fire damage.

Twin Assault 1/Long Rest
When you take the Attack action, the drake may make one free attack. If both of your attacks hit the same target this turn, the drake's attack deals maximum fire damage.

Inferno Ascendant 1/Long Rest action, 1 minute
Invokes Varmyrr's calm flame. For the duration: the drake's attacks deal an extra 1d6 fire damage, the drake gains one extra attack when it takes the Attack action, and your Dragon Breath recharge check (see Actions) becomes a 5–6.

Tranquil Roar when Inferno Ascendant ends
The drake releases a calming roar. Enemies of your choice within 30 feet make a DC 15 Wisdom save or are frightened until the end of their next turn. Allies in that range gain 1d6 + your Wisdom modifier temporary hit points.

Minor Boon of Adore — Freedom of Form

Boon · the drake shifts form, but Lyrielle spends the Wild Shape use

action + 1 Wild shape use (Lyrielle), 1 hour or until dusk
Transform the drake into a CR 0 Commoner stat block. In this form it appears as a slightly feral duplicate of Lyrielle, keeping a few visible draconic tells: draconic horns, slitted draconic eyes, and patches of visible scale.

The drake keeps its own alignment, personality, and mental ability scores, but takes on the Commoner's physical ability scores.

Duration. Until the end of the drake's next short rest — or until the next dusk, if it eats a full meal before dusk while transformed. It reverts early if the effect is dismissed (no action required) or if it falls unconscious.

Saddle of the Cavalier

Uncommon · tack, worn by the drake

While in this saddle on a mount, you can't be dismounted against your will if you're conscious, and attack rolls against the mount have disadvantage.

Horseshoes of Speed

Rare · set of four, affixed to a mount's feet

A set of 4. As a Magic action, you can touch one of the horseshoes to the hoof of a horse or similar creature, whereupon the horseshoe affixes itself to the hoof. Removing a horseshoe also takes a Magic action. While all four horseshoes are attached to the same creature, its Speed is increased by 30 feet (included in statistics).

Wraps of Unarmed Power +3

Worn · bonus folded directly into the Bite attack above

While wearing these wraps, you have a bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with your Unarmed Strikes. The bonus is determined by the wraps’ rarity, and those strikes deal your choice of Force damage or their normal damage type. The bonus is already included in the character stats.

Rider Synergy — Mounted Combatant

LV8 — Mounted Combatant Feat. Works in tandem for Drake and Lyrielle

Mounted Strike. While mounted, Lyrielle has advantage on attack rolls against any unmounted creature within 5 feet of the drake that is at least one size smaller than it.

Leap Aside. If the drake is subjected to an effect that lets it make a Dexterity save for half damage, it takes no damage on a success and half on a failure instead — as long as Lyrielle is riding it and neither is incapacitated.

Veer. While mounted, Lyrielle can force an attack that hits the drake to hit her instead, if she isn't incapacitated.
